Grading Rubric

Each ETF is scored 1–5 on six dimensions, weighted, and summed to a percentage. Rev. 2: diversification now carries the most weight; AI-theme purity carries the least. Disagree with a weight? The raw 1–5 scores are shown on every card — re-weight by eye.

DimensionWeightWhat it measures
Diversification35% ↑ was 20%Holdings count & weighting method — how much rides on any single company. Now the dominant factor.
Cost15%Expense ratio — lower is better, compounds over a 2–3yr hold
Liquidity15%Average daily volume — thin volume means wider spreads, worse fills
Risk, calmer15%52-week price swing — narrower range scores higher (easier to hold through)
Momentum15%Current price's position within its 52-week range — closer to the high scores higher
AI purity5% ↓ was 20%How directly the fund is exposed to the AI-buildout thesis. Demoted — concentration in one theme is a risk to weigh elsewhere (diversification), not a score booster.
