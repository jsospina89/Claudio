How they stack up

  1. SMH — best balance: same AI purity as SOXX, but calmer 52-week swing (128% vs. 165%) and stronger momentum right now. The modest concentration in Nvidia/TSM-type names cuts both ways — it's also why it isn't a 5/5 on diversification.
  2. SOXX — near-identical thesis to SMH with the widest 52-week swing of the three. If you already hold SMH, this doesn't add much diversification — it's close to a duplicate.
  3. XSD — the genuine diversification play (equal-weight, no single mega-cap dominance) but its average daily volume (92K vs. millions for the other two) means wider bid-ask spreads and tougher fills at size. Worth it only if the equal-weight exposure specifically matters to you.

Practical note: SMH and SOXX are close enough in construction that holding both adds cost without much real diversification — pick one.

How they stack up

  1. VGT — cheapest, purest tech exposure, and currently the strongest momentum of the three. The tradeoff is a wider 52-week swing than QQQ or MAGS — it's 100% tech, with no non-tech Nasdaq names (Costco, Pepsi, etc.) to smooth things out.
  2. QQQ — the steadiest all-rounder: cheap, extremely liquid, real historical track record (3yr Sharpe 1.07). Slightly diluted AI exposure since ~40% of it isn't tech.
  3. MAGS — this is the one where the grade undersells the concentration risk: 1/5 on diversification is doing a lot of work. It scores well on "calmness" and "AI purity" specifically because these seven companies have been remarkably stable lately, but a single-name blowup (one bad earnings call from any of the seven) moves 1/7 of the whole fund at once. Fits the "steadier than individual picks, but not steady in the way QQQ or VGT are" framing from earlier.

Note the surprise: MAGS currently shows the narrowest 52-week swing of these three. Equal-weighting seven mega-caps has been calm recently — that's a snapshot of the last year, not a permanent trait of concentrated bets.

Grading Rubric

Each ETF is scored 1–5 on six dimensions, weighted, and summed to a percentage. Disagree with a weight? The raw 1–5 scores are shown on every card — re-weight by eye.

DimensionWeightWhat it measures
Cost15%Expense ratio — lower is better, compounds over a 2–3yr hold
Liquidity15%Average daily volume — thin volume means wider spreads, worse fills
Diversification20%Holdings count & weighting method — how much rides on any single company
AI purity20%How directly the fund is exposed to the AI-buildout thesis
Risk (calmer)15%52-week price swing — narrower range scores higher (easier to hold through)
Momentum15%Current price's position within its 52-week range — closer to the high scores higher
