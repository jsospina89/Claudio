The honest takeaway

This rubric rewards cost, liquidity, diversification, calm, and momentum — it has no way to measure "how AI-specific is this fund," so a generic sector SPDR can out-score a purpose-built thematic ETF while being less relevant to the actual thesis (XLI, XLRE). The one case where the sector fund is a clean, no-compromise upgrade is XLU over GRID/NLR — utilities directly capture the power-demand story without dilution. The others are a real tradeoff: cheaper and calmer, at the cost of precision.
